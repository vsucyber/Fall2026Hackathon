How It Works

From challenge to final pitch

The hackathon is structured around a simple progression so students can focus on building and communicating a strong solution.

01

Discover the Challenge

Review the announced application topics, identify a meaningful problem, and understand the people affected by it.

02

Form Your Idea

Define your users, the problem you will solve, and the core features that make your solution useful and distinctive.

03

Design & Prototype

Create the workflow, interface, and prototype that demonstrate how your application would work in practice.

04

Test the Story

Refine usability, remove unnecessary complexity, and make sure your prototype clearly addresses the original problem.

05

Prepare the Pitch

Explain the problem, your solution, why it matters, and what makes your approach innovative and practical.

06

Present & Celebrate

Showcase your work to faculty, peers, and judges and learn from solutions developed by teams across institutions.

Judging

What makes a strong solution?

Use these qualities to guide your solution from the first idea through the final presentation.

💡
Innovation

How creative, distinctive, and thoughtful is the proposed solution?

🎯
Problem Fit

Does the solution address a clear and meaningful real-world need?

🧭
Usability & Design

Is the experience understandable, accessible, and visually coherent?

🚀
Feasibility

Could the concept realistically be developed and used beyond the prototype?

🛠️
Prototype Quality

Does the prototype clearly demonstrate the essential features and workflow?

🎤
Presentation

Can the team clearly communicate the problem, solution, value, and impact?

FAQ

Common questions

Who can participate?

Students from Virginia State University, National Taipei University, NED University, Brightpoint Community College, Northern Virginia Community College (NOVA), and Binghamton University may participate. Teams are encouraged to include students from more than one university.

Do I need to be an expert programmer?

No. The hackathon welcomes students with varied technical backgrounds. Strong teams combine problem solving, application design, prototyping, teamwork, technical thinking, and presentation skills.

When will the application topics be released?

The challenge problem set will be released on November 7, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. EST (Virginia time). A PDF link will appear automatically at that time.

When are registration and submission deadlines?

The hackathon will run from November 7–14, 2026. Registration is now open. Submission requirements and presentation details will be provided with the challenge topics
